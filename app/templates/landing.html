Core Features

What Resume.AI does

Six things a keyword ATS cannot. All defensible in a viva.

01 / 06

Semantic matching

Sentence-BERT (MiniLM-L6-v2) encodes each resume and JD into 384-dim vectors, so "TensorFlow" still ranks for a "deep learning" JD.

02 / 06

Two-stage retrieval

FAISS surfaces the top 40 candidates in milliseconds. A cross-encoder then reads each JD-resume pair jointly and produces the final ranking.

03 / 06

Fixes the 80% experience gap

Regex + spaCy correctly extract years of experience where the baseline ATS defaulted to 0 for 8 out of 10 candidates in our tests.

04 / 06

Explainable scores

Every candidate breaks down into semantic, skills, experience and education sub-scores — with matched and missing skills listed. No black box.

05 / 06

Bias-check mode

One toggle strips names, emails and phone numbers before scoring, so hiring decisions can be defended as identity-blind.

06 / 06

Batch-first workflow

Drop a ZIP of 500 PDFs or DOCX files. Set a top-N cutoff. Download a clean ranked CSV. Built for the real recruiter loop.

How it works

Four stages
honest scores.

No fabricated data. No hidden weights. Every number is the model's actual output.

Stage 01

Parse

PDF, DOCX, TXT or a ZIP folder. Text extracted and normalised. Failed files are flagged, never silently dropped.

Stage 02

Extract

Skills lexicon + regex + spaCy pull skills, years of experience, and degree family from resume and JD.

Stage 03

Retrieve

MiniLM embeddings + FAISS cosine search shortlists the strongest candidates in milliseconds.

Stage 04

Rerank & score

Cross-encoder rereads each pair jointly. A weighted scorer combines semantic, skills, experience and education.
